Agents are a Language Model autonomously pursues a goal by selecting actions, invoking tools, and iterating until the goal is reached. It perceives context, selects actions, invokes tools, and iterates until finish.

## Autonomous Agent
An agent that operates without step-by-step human direction, making its own decisions to reach the goal. It plans the steps to achieve the high level goal.

## What Makes a System Agentic?
The model controls the flow at inference time. It means that they have the capability to interact with the world (Tool use), and can adapt to the different findings during the process time (Do not depend on only one path like Workflows).
